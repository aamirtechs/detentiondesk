Local first. Your data stays in this browser.

Detention pay you earned should not quietly disappear.

Log every detention, TONU, layover, lumper and extra stop. DetentionDesk does the math, tracks the claim from unbilled to paid, flags money that is slipping away, and builds a clean claim packet in one click.

The problem

Small fleets lose detention money because waits are scribbled on paper, free time terms live in old rate cons, and nobody follows up after a claim is denied.

The fix

One ledger per load, automatic owed math, a status pipeline, red flags after 48 hours unbilled, and rankings that show which facilities and brokers cost you most.

No account, no subscription

No sign up, no paid API, no server. Everything saves to localStorage on this device. Export CSV any time for your own records.
